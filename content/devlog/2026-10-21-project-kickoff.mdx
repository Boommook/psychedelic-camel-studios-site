> This is a sample development entry. It describes the initial brainstorming phase, not completed milestones or confirmed gameplay.

## Before the world takes shape

We are Psychedelic Camel Studios, a student team at Worcester Polytechnic Institute working on a game for Technical Game Development II. Our team brings together five technical developers and two artists, using Unreal Engine.

Our project is in brainstorming and preproduction. Even the working title is temporary. This space will document the decisions, experiments, and questions that emerge throughout the term.

## Questions we are exploring

The leading direction is a surreal, potentially horror-inspired puzzle/adventure about altered perception. We are considering questions such as:

- What might change when the same environment is perceived differently?
- How could familiar spaces become unfamiliar?
- Could environmental inconsistencies invite exploration and observation?
- What role might impossible geometry, mirrors, or distorted time play?

These are possibilities to investigate, not a feature list. The concept, perspective, and mechanics remain open.

## What this journal is for

Future entries can share design reasoning, prototype observations, visual experiments, and coursework artifacts when they exist. We will distinguish proposals from decisions and describe the project as it actually develops.
